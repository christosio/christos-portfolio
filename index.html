05 / BEYOND THE SCREEN

Competing with purpose.

Boccia is a Paralympic precision sport. Players throw red or blue balls and try to finish closer to the white target ball, called the jack, than their opponent. An individual match has four ends, with six balls for each player in every end.

I compete in the BC1 class, where athletes play with their hands or feet and may have a sport assistant to help with tasks such as positioning the wheelchair and passing balls. Every throw calls for accuracy, planning and adaptability.

Boccia has grown in Cyprus over the past four years, and I am proud to represent the country at international events. Training and competition have strengthened my focus, preparation and ability to adapt under pressure.
